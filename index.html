Now building

Tessera Build Inc., Co-founder (pre-launch)

Tessera Build Inc. is an early-stage company I co-founded. Its vision is an intelligence and orchestration layer for the home: a verified home passport that begins with the builder's records and stays with the house through every owner. The goal is for homeowners to set up services, see what's under warranty, and reach the right trade from one place, and for builders to spend less time on calls and email. The idea draws on my earlier work at AWS unifying security findings across AWS accounts into one trusted view.
